Questions

Is it free?

Yes, while we're in early access: every feature, no limits on people. When paid plans come, the price will be on this page, one simple per-person price with every module included.

Does it run payroll?

No. Payroll is the most complained-about part of HR suites, so we don't pretend to do it. You get a clean payroll export for any period (pay, changes, paid and unpaid days off) to hand to your payroll provider or accountant.

Who can see my salary or personal details?

Your pay: you, the managers above you and HR. Your personal details (address, phone, emergency contact, documents): you and HR. Colleagues see your name, job, team, work email and birthday (day and month only).

Are the pulse surveys really anonymous?

Answers are stored without any link to who gave them; the app only records that you answered so it doesn't ask twice. Results stay hidden until at least three people answer.

Can I get my data out?

Any time. Settings has a one-click export of your whole company, and Reports has CSV files of people and payroll.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in any modern browser, and you can add it to your home screen.
